## Setup and accessibility

- Before doors open, V4 verifies a continuous step-free route from the arrival point through entry, event seating, refreshments, and the accessible restroom. V6 arranges seating with clear aisles, integrated wheelchair spaces, companion seats, and seats with a clear view of the speakers. V4 checks the layout after setup. If the route fails the check, V4 coordinates a verified accessible entrance or room with the venue and directs arrivals there. V1 holds admission until an accessible route is available.
- V4 checks requests sent to [Accessibility contact] before the event and takes requests at entry or during the event. Share the verified route and accommodation contact through [RSVP link] and at check-in. Make the agenda and prompts available as plain text on request and in large print at the venue. V1 reads schedule changes aloud, and V2 makes them available in writing.
- Attendees can stay seated throughout networking and have partners rotate to them, use spoken or written prompts, observe, or take a quiet break. Sharing contact details is optional. V3 asks panelists to speak one at a time and repeat audience questions before answering.
- V5 labels each food option with its name and any ingredient and allergen information available from the packaging. Keep wrappers available for checking, display fruit separately by type, and flag missing information as unknown. V2 keeps drink labels visible. V4 can bring labeled choices to attendees at their seats.

## Contingencies

| Owner | Trigger | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V6 | Projector or Wi-Fi fails at setup or during the event | Stop troubleshooting once the affected activity is due to start. Signal V3 to continue the panel as a moderated discussion, direct attendees to the printed agenda and prompts already at hand, and use spoken timing cues for networking. |
| V3 | A speaker cancels or is absent at 16:35 | Confirm the remaining speakers and redistribute questions. If no speaker is available, run a facilitated peer discussion in the panel slot using the printed prompts on career paths, skills, and next steps. |
| V1 | A volunteer misses the pre-opening check or leaves mid-event | Reassign essential duties before the next block. If V4 is absent, move V6 to access support and switch to printed cues. If V5 is absent, move V2 to the food station during the break and use one station for both food and drinks. If V3 is absent, V1 moderates and V2 keeps time. If V2 is absent, fold questions into the moderated discussion and use one refreshment station. If V6 is absent, use printed cues and keep groups seated. If V1 is absent, V3 takes this coordination duty and calls transitions. |
| V5 | Setup count falls below 50 usable servings of food or drinks, or stock runs low before everyone is served | Count staged stock and open the held surplus of 10 food and 22 drink servings as needed. If the count still falls short, alert V1 to arrange whole packs of FRUIT10 or WATER24 from the reserve if delivery by the break is feasible. During service, pause seconds and distribute available first servings to attendees still waiting. If replenishment is unavailable, V1 announces the shortage and any venue-confirmed drinking-water option. |
