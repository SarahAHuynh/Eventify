## Coverage and operating assumptions

- The planned mix provides 60 food servings and 72 bottled drinks, enough for one of each per student, with 10 food and 22 drink servings held as surplus. Offer first servings before releasing extras.
- Two tag packs cover 50 students. The third pack provides 25 extra tags. V2 uses the eight markers as shared supplies at check-in and moves them to written participation activities as needed.
- The print allocation assumes one double-sided attendee agenda counts as one copy in a PRINT25 batch and that the same batch can include signs. Logistics should confirm those details before ordering. The icebreaker and closing prompts should use the attendee agendas. Any separately distributed prompt sheets would draw from the 25-copy allocation for signs or backups and should be reconciled in the supply table.

## Refreshment shortage procedure

1. V5 counts usable stock and stages first servings and surplus separately before opening. V2 takes over counting, staging, food labeling, and drink-label checks if V5 is absent. If V5 leaves during service, V6 pauses the queue while V2 consolidates stock at the combined station, reconciles servings already distributed, stages remaining usable stock, and rechecks food and drink labels. V2 clears V6 to resume the queue and tracks first food and drink servings at the combined station. V4 continues seated delivery from the combined station. During networking, V6 holds release until V2 confirms both label checks when covering for V5.

   Follow the [Logistics r3 opening desk handoff](../logistics-r3/logistics.md#opening-desk-handoff-when-v5-is-absent) when V2 handles stock during welcome. The desk owner checks attendees in, distributes agendas, and keeps self-service tags and markers available. V6 gives seat directions from the desk while covering it. V4 stays at the accessibility point.

   - **Both absences known before opening.** V3 assigns V6 to the desk. V6 confirms readiness to V3 before 16:00 and covers the first arrivals while V2 counts and stages stock. If V2 finishes before opening, V2 confirms readiness to V6 and opens the desk.
   - **Absences overlap during welcome.** If V2 is covering the desk, V2 stays there, signals V3, and waits for V6 to say “I have check-in” in person before leaving to handle stock. This applies before and after the 16:22 cue. If V1 leaves while V2 is already doing stock work, V6 takes ownership immediately and confirms the transfer to V3. If V6 already owns the desk, coverage continues.
   - **V1 remains on duty.** V1 stations V6 alongside the desk before V2 steps away for stock work or doors open with stock work underway. At 16:22, V2 accepts the desk if back. If stock work is still underway, V6 accepts before V1 leaves to give the welcome.
   - **V2 returns.** After counting and staging stock and making any immediate deficit report, V2 takes a briefing from V6 and says “I have check-in” before V6 leaves. If V1 still owns the desk before 16:22, V2 resumes tag support until accepting the desk at 16:22. If V1 is absent and V6 still owns the desk at 16:38, V6 signals V2 and V3 and stays with arrivals. V2 pauses stock work to brief and stage speakers by 16:42, then finishes the stock work and accepts the desk in person. If V6 is still covering arrivals at 16:45, V3 starts the panel in discussion format.
2. Start the shortage response if usable food or drinks fall below 50 servings before service or remaining stock cannot cover outstanding first servings during service. V5, or V2 if V5 is absent, counts usable stock against outstanding first servings, releases usable held surplus as needed up to the planned 10 food and 22 drink servings, and recounts. Hold seconds and prioritize attendees still waiting for first servings, including those receiving seated service from V4. Report any remaining deficit immediately by item to V1, or to V3 if V1 is absent. When both V1 and V5 are absent, V2 reports directly to V3.
3. V1 or acting lead V3 authorizes replenishment if it can arrive in time to cover first servings. Round each remaining item deficit up separately to whole packs. Use FRUIT10 for food at 1000 cents per 10 servings and WATER24 for drinks at 800 cents per 24 servings. Multiply the pack counts by their catalog prices and deduct that amount from the remaining reserve. The cumulative draw can be at most **13400 cents**, leaving at least **3000 cents** in reserve and keeping purchases plus remaining reserve within **30000 cents**.
4. V5, or V2 when covering V5, checks replacement food labels for available ingredient and allergen information and verifies drink labels are legible and visible before serving. If replenishment is unavailable, V1 or acting lead V3 announces the shortage and any venue-confirmed drinking-water option. Keep seconds on hold while first servings are outstanding.
