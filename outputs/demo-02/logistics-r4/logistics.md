## Setup and accessibility

- Before doors open, V4 verifies a continuous step-free route from the arrival point through entry, event seating, refreshments, and the accessible restroom. V6 arranges seating with clear aisles, integrated wheelchair spaces, companion seats, and seats with a clear view of the speakers. V4 checks the layout after setup. If the route fails the check, V4 coordinates a verified accessible entrance or room with the venue and directs arrivals there. V1 holds admission until an accessible route is available. V3 makes the admission decision if V1 is absent.
- V4 checks requests sent to [Accessibility contact] before the event and takes requests at entry or during the event. Share the verified route and accommodation contact through [RSVP link] and at check-in. Make the agenda and prompts available as plain text on request and in large print at the venue. V1 reads schedule changes aloud, and V2 makes them available in writing. If V2 is absent, V6 handles written updates as described below. If V1 is absent, the volunteer calling transitions in that block reads changes aloud.
- Attendees can stay seated throughout networking and have partners rotate to them, use spoken or written prompts, observe, or take a quiet break. Sharing contact details is optional. V3 asks panelists to speak one at a time and repeat audience questions before answering.
- Before doors open, V5 counts and stages usable stock and sets aside the planned surplus. During networking, V5 labels each food option with its name and any ingredient and allergen information available from the packaging. Keep wrappers available for checking, display fruit separately by type, and flag missing information as unknown. V5 checks that drink labels are legible and visible before V6 releases attendees. V2 keeps them visible during service. If V5 is absent, V2 takes over the count, staging, food labels, and drink-label check on the schedule below. V4 can bring labeled choices to attendees at their seats.

## Contingencies

| Owner | Trigger | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V6 | Projector or Wi-Fi fails at setup or during the event | Stop troubleshooting once the affected activity is due to start. Signal V3 to continue the panel as a moderated discussion, direct attendees to the printed agenda and prompts already at hand, and use spoken timing cues for networking. |
| V3 | A speaker cancels or is absent at 16:35 | Confirm the remaining speakers and redistribute questions. If no speaker is available, run a facilitated peer discussion in the panel slot using the printed prompts on career paths, skills, and next steps. |
| V1, or V3 if V1 is absent | A volunteer misses the pre-opening check or leaves mid-event | Make the handoff as soon as the absence is known. Use the schedules below for V1 and V5. If V4 is absent, move V6 to access support and switch to printed cues. If V3 is absent, V1 moderates and V2 keeps time. If V2 is absent, use the desk, written-update, panel, and refreshment handoffs below. If V6 is absent, use printed cues and keep groups seated. |
| V5, or V2 if V5 is absent | The count falls below 50 usable food or drink servings before service, or remaining stock cannot cover outstanding first servings during service | Count usable stock against outstanding first servings. Release usable held surplus as needed (planned at 10 food and 22 drinks) and recount. Hold seconds and prioritize attendees still waiting for first servings, including V4’s seated-service requests. Immediately report any remaining deficit by item to V1, or V3 if V1 is absent. The receiving lead arranges whole packs of FRUIT10 or WATER24 from the reserve if they can arrive in time, rounding each item deficit up separately at 1000 cents per 10 fruit servings and 800 cents per 24 drinks. The cumulative reserve draw may be at most 13400 cents and must leave at least 3000 cents. V5, or V2 if V5 is absent, checks replacement food for available ingredient and allergen information and verifies drink labels are visible and legible before serving. If replenishment is unavailable, the lead announces the shortage and any venue-confirmed drinking-water option. |
