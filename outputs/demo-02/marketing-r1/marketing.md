## Promotional email

**Subject:** RSVP for Women in Tech: Connect & Grow — February 19, 2027

**Body:**

Hi there,

You are invited to Women in Tech: Connect & Grow, a college student event at [Venue] on Friday, February 19, 2027, 16:00–19:00 local time. There is room for 50 college students.

Here is how the afternoon will run:

- **16:00–16:25 — Welcome and check-in.** Pick up your name tag and agenda, find a seat, and hear the welcome.
- **16:25–16:45 — Icebreaker.** In seated pairs, share a technology interest or a skill you want to learn. Writing, listening, and passing are welcome.
- **16:45–17:30 — Career panel.** The planned panel includes introductions, a moderated discussion, and time for attendee questions.
- **17:30–18:15 — Structured networking.** Three 10-minute rounds on interests, career questions, and next steps. Sharing contact details is optional.
- **18:15–18:40 — Refreshment break.** One food serving and one drink serving per attendee.
- **18:40–19:00 — Closing activity.** Choose one next step, hear the recap, and head out by 19:00.

Need an accommodation to participate? Contact [Accessibility contact].

Ready to join us? RSVP at [RSVP link].

See you there,
The Women in Tech: Connect & Grow team
