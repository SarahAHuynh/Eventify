## Accessibility and setup

Before doors open, V1 confirms [Venue], room access and opening time, and V3 checks the step-free entrance and route, accessible restrooms, aisles, integrated accessible seating and a low-stimulation seating area. V4 checks projector and Wi-Fi. Share the confirmed entrance and route with attendees. Keep routes clear through check-in, seating and food service. Offer the printed large-print handout and an accessible digital text version. Read prompts aloud, face the room, and repeat audience questions before answering. Offer seated networking, quiet participation and seated refreshment service. Keep snack ingredient and allergen labels visible, serve fruit separately and confirm any dietary needs before buying.

Direct accommodation requests to [Accessibility contact]. V3 follows up privately, confirms requested arrangements with campus or venue support, and tells the attendee what is available. Confirm any requested communication services and funding before promising them.

## Contingencies

| Trigger | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Speaker cancels | Cover the remaining panelists with prepared questions. If all cancel, use the panel slot for a facilitated career-path discussion and Q&A with prepared prompts. | V4 |
| Projector or Wi-Fi fails | Use offline files and the printed prompts. Read key information aloud and use the offline RSVP roster. | V4 for AV, V2 for check-in |
| One volunteer is absent | Combine registration and floor direction after check-in, simplify rotations, and cross-cover. V4 backs up facilitation, V6 backs up supplies, and V1 assigns coverage before doors. Preserve a designated accessibility lead with V2 backing up V3. | V1 |
| 50 attendees admitted | Close walk-in admission, use a waitlist before doors for released RSVP places, and keep the exit route clear. | V2 |
| Short delivery or dietary need identified | Check counts and labels before doors. Use reserve for eligible catalog replacement packs if needed. Confirm ingredient suitability with the attendee and campus support if the catalog cannot meet a disclosed need. | V5 with V3 |
| Step-free route, room or requested accommodation is unavailable | Work with campus staff to arrange an accessible route, room or equivalent accommodation. If the venue cannot support safe access, pause admission, notify registrants and coordinate relocation or rescheduling. | V3 with V1 |
| Agenda runs late | Shorten panel Q&A or networking transitions as appropriate, preserving the six blocks in order, the refreshment break and the 19:00 close. | V1 |
